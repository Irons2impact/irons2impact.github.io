Complete these details as agreed by both parties. The term has no automatic renewal. Deliverable fees must add up to the agreed total cash compensation.

2. Agreement terms

2 Promotional services

Athlete will perform only the services described in Exhibit A for the actual business purpose stated there. Exhibit A must identify the products or services promoted, deliverables, platforms, dates, location, content requirements and compensation allocated to each deliverable. Added services require both parties’ written agreement and any additional compensation.

3 Compensation and expenses

Sponsor will pay Athlete total cash compensation of $____________ and provide the following noncash compensation and agreed value: ________________________________. Payment dates, milestones and method must be entered in Exhibit A. No commission, deduction or fee applies unless separately agreed in a lawful written agreement.

Sponsor will pay approved, properly completed deliverables within 15 calendar days after the applicable milestone and receipt of an invoice, unless Exhibit A states an earlier date. Sponsor must identify a specific deficiency within five business days of delivery; otherwise the deliverable is accepted. Athlete receives a reasonable opportunity to correct any deficiency.

Only expenses approved in writing in advance are reimbursable. Approved expenses are due within 15 calendar days after receipts are submitted. Each party is responsible for its own taxes and legally required reporting. Tax information must be exchanged securely.

4 Limited NIL license and content

Athlete grants Sponsor a nonexclusive, nontransferable license during the term to use Athlete’s approved name, image, likeness, voice and biographical details solely for the campaign, media and territory identified in Exhibit A. Athlete retains all NIL rights. Sponsor may allow campaign vendors to use approved material only to perform this Agreement, subject to the same limits; Sponsor remains responsible for their use.

Athlete retains ownership of Athlete-created content and licenses approved content only within the limits above. Sponsor retains ownership of independently created production materials, subject to Athlete’s NIL rights and this Agreement. Neither party may use another owner’s photographs, music, footage, school logos, uniforms or trademarks without necessary permission.

Athlete must approve each proposed advertisement, material edit and context of use in writing before publication. Silence is not approval. No sublicensing, merchandise, paid advertising, account access, digital replicas, synthetic voice, AI training or materially altered likeness is permitted without a separate written agreement. Allowed media and any paid advertising must be expressly identified in Exhibit A.

Within 10 business days after expiration or termination, Sponsor must stop new use and remove campaign material from channels it controls. Legally required records and inaccessible internal archives may be retained without public or promotional use. Any extension requires a new written agreement.

5 Eligibility and truthful advertising

Compensation is solely for legitimate NIL services and rights. It is not conditioned on athletic statistics, playing time, competition results, admission, enrollment, transfer, recruiting decisions or attendance at a particular school. Neither party guarantees scholarships, admission, roster positions, NIL income or professional opportunities.

Both parties must comply with applicable law and school, association, conference and governing athletics requirements. Athlete will make required disclosures and obtain any required reviews or permissions before performance. Sponsor will promptly provide complete deal and payment information and cooperate with compliance review, including NIL Go where applicable. Confidentiality does not prevent required disclosure or consultation with legal, tax, parental or compliance advisers.

Athlete will clearly disclose the paid relationship in endorsements as required by law and platform rules. Statements must reflect Athlete’s honest experience; Sponsor is responsible for substantiating product claims it supplies. No unlawful or prohibited product or campaign may be required.

6 Scheduling and relationship

Services must not interfere with required academic, team or competition obligations. Injury, illness and schedule conflicts will be addressed through reasonable rescheduling or mutually approved substitute services. Injury or transfer alone does not forfeit earned compensation.

Athlete acts as an independent contractor to the extent permitted by law. This Agreement does not appoint Sponsor as Athlete’s agent, authorize negotiation with schools, or authorize Sponsor to bind Athlete. No exclusivity applies unless a separate signed addendum defines the narrow category, duration and additional compensation.

7 Breach and termination

Either party may terminate for a material breach that remains uncured 10 calendar days after written notice describing the breach. Either party may immediately suspend affected activity if a school compliance office or governing authority identifies a credible eligibility or legal concern. The parties will try in good faith to modify the affected terms; if unresolved within 15 calendar days, either may terminate the affected obligations in writing.

On termination, Sponsor must pay for completed, conforming services and approved noncancelable expenses. Athlete must return only advances allocated to unperformed services, within 30 calendar days after a written accounting. No repayment of earned compensation or penalty is due solely because of injury, transfer, eligibility review or termination. The NIL license ends as provided in Section 4.

8 Responsibility and disputes

Each party is responsible for its own breach, negligence, unlawful conduct and unauthorized use of third-party rights, subject to applicable law. Neither party provides a blanket release or indemnity under this template. Any additional liability allocation requires a separately negotiated written provision.

The parties will attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith discussions for 15 calendar days after written notice, except where urgent court relief is needed. Governing law and court venue are specified in the completed campaign details, subject to mandatory applicable law. This Agreement does not require arbitration or waive statutory rights.

9 Notices and complete agreement

Notices must be sent to the email addresses below. Termination and breach notices must also be sent to the postal addresses in Section 1. Email notice is effective upon acknowledgment or other confirmed delivery. Each party must keep contact information current.

This Agreement and signed exhibits are the entire agreement on this campaign. Amendments must be in writing signed by both parties. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other’s written consent. An unenforceable provision will be limited only to the extent law permits; remaining provisions continue. Electronic signatures and counterparts are permitted to the extent allowed by law. Payment, ownership, use restrictions and dispute provisions survive as needed to enforce accrued rights.

3. Your signature

For a minor athlete, guardian signing does not replace any required approvals or other protections described in the original agreement. Have the completed terms reviewed before use.
