WHAT WE COLLECT

The inquiry form requests basic athlete and contact information, program interest, and optional notes. It does not automatically send that information to a server or save it to a website database.

HOW INQUIRIES WORK

When you use the email preparation feature, the website opens your device's email application with a prepared message addressed to the organization's configured contact email. You decide whether to send it. If you download an inquiry, a text file is created on your device. Email providers may process messages according to their own policies.

MINORS

For athletes under 18, a parent or authorized guardian should complete or supervise any inquiry. Please do not send sensitive medical, school-record, financial, or identification information through the form.

EXTERNAL SERVICES

The site loads optional fonts from Google Fonts. If those resources are blocked, your device's system fonts are used. Standard web hosting may generate routine server logs. Website analytics, trackers, and advertising cookies are not intentionally installed in this package.

RETENTION AND CONTACT

Any information you choose to send by email will be handled by the organization and its email service. Before publishing, the site owner should provide a verified contact email and review its own record-retention and privacy policies.

NOT A CONTRACT

Submitting an inquiry does not create an athlete representation relationship or guarantee program participation, endorsement deals, recruiting outcomes, scholarships, or enrollment.
